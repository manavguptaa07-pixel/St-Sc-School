Academics

Four learning stages, each designed to build the skills a child needs next.

Stage

Foundation Stage

Learning begins with play. Stories, songs, rhymes and activities help young children build language, number sense and good habits in a happy setting.

  • Play-based learning
  • Early reading and number skills
  • Art, music and movement
Stage

Preparatory Stage

Children grow into confident readers and writers while exploring the world through simple projects and discussion.

  • Language and mathematics foundations
  • Environmental awareness
  • Creative projects and group activities
Stage

Middle Stage

Subjects become deeper and more connected. Experiments, discussions and teamwork make concepts clear and memorable.

  • Science, mathematics and languages
  • Social science and computer learning
  • Quizzes, models and presentations
Stage

Secondary Stage

Focused guidance, regular practice and personal mentoring prepare students for board examinations and future studies.

  • Exam-oriented practice and revision
  • Career guidance and counselling
  • Regular tests and parent meetings
